{% trans "Is this your stand?" %}

{% blocktrans %}HiloBakeStands.com is a free local directory of bake stands, food trucks, farm stands, and pop-ups around Hilo — a simple way for locals and visitors to find ono grindz, see when places are open, and get directions. If you run a stand we listed, you can claim it and take over the details yourself.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What claiming gets you" %}

{% blocktrans %}It's completely free, and there's nothing to buy. Once a listing is yours, you set your hours, flip an open/closed-today switch, add photos and specials, and update your contact links and payment options. Most owners just point people to their socials — that's fine too.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Don't want to be listed?" %}

{% blocktrans %}That's okay — claim your stand and you can hide it, or get in touch and we'll take it down. One honest heads-up: because anyone can suggest a stand, a community member could add yours again later. Claiming it is the surest way to stay in control of how (or whether) it shows up.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "How to claim it" %}

{% blocktrans %}Claiming is owner-initiated, so just reach out and we'll figure it out together — there's no form to wrestle with. Two easy ways:{% endblocktrans %}

  • {% blocktrans %}Email matt@hilobakestands.com and let us know which stand is yours.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Or message us from your business socials to @hilobakestands on Instagram — a DM from your own account is an easy way to show the stand is really yours.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}We're also making the rounds in person — if we haven't reached you yet, we may swing by and leave a claim slip at your cashbox as we get around town.{% endblocktrans %}
