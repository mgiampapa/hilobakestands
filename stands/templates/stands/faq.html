{% trans "Frequently asked questions" %}

{% trans "What is this?" %}

{% blocktrans %}HiloBakeStands is a free directory of Hilo's bake stands, farm stands, food trucks, and pop-ups — the little spots that announce "we're open!" on Instagram and sell out by noon. It's a map and a list so locals and visitors can find them, see today's hours, and get directions. That's it.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Is it free?" %}

{% blocktrans %}Yes. It's not Yelp. There are no ads, no fees, no "claim your page for $X/month," and we don't sell your information. It's a community project, run by one person who likes good food.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "How do I list my bake stand / farm stand / food truck / pop-up?" %}

{% blocktrans %}A few ways:{% endblocktrans %}

  • {% blocktrans %}Use the "Add a stand" link at the top of the site — it takes a minute.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Email matt@hilobakestands.com.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}DM @hilobakestands on Instagram from your business account.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}You don't have to do anything special — if someone already added your stand, you can just claim it (see below). New submissions get a quick look before they go live.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What data do you collect?" %}

{% blocktrans %}As little as possible. Your listing is public business info you'd put on a flyer anyway — name, what you sell, hours, location, payment types, social links. For visitors: no cookies, no Google Analytics, no tracking across sites. We keep basic, anonymized counts of page visits to know what's useful, but we never store your raw IP address and can't tie visits back to you. If you sign in to manage your stand, that's through Google and we only see your name and email. We don't sell anything to anyone.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "My stand is already listed — how do I manage it?" %}

{% url 'claim_your_stand' as claim_url %}

{% blocktrans %}Claim it. On your stand's page, tap "🤙 Is this your stand?" (or visit the Claim your stand page). You'll sign in with Google, and once it's yours you get an owner dashboard where you can edit your details, hours, map pin, and photos. If you got a flyer with a QR code or link from Matthew, that link claims it directly. Stuck? Email matt@hilobakestands.com.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "I don't have a fixed schedule — how do I mark my stand Open or Closed?" %}

{% blocktrans %}That's exactly what the site is built for. From your dashboard, tap a button for today: We're open today, Closed today, Pau! (sold out), or Regular schedule. It updates your listing instantly — open stands show up in the "Open now" filter, and "Pau!" shows a "sold out" note so people know to catch you next time. It resets on its own at midnight, so you just tap a button each morning you're open. No fixed hours required.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "I'm taking a break, stopping, or just don't want to be listed — how do I remove my stand?" %}

{% blocktrans %}If you've claimed your stand, go to "Edit details" and switch your visibility to Hidden. It disappears from the list, map, and "Open now" right away, and you can flip it back to Listed anytime — handy if you're just taking a break or closing for the season.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}One heads-up: hiding isn't the same as deleting. Because anyone in the community can add a stand they love, a hidden stand can get re-submitted by a fan down the road. If you want your stand and its records permanently removed rather than just hidden, email matt@hilobakestands.com and Matthew will take care of it.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What if there's a problem with a stand?" %}

{% blocktrans %}Couldn't find it, the hours looked off, it wasn't actually open, something's out of date — let us know. Every stand's page has a "Report a problem" link; send a quick note and it goes straight to Matthew to check and fix. Reporting a stand never deletes it or gets anyone in trouble — it just helps keep the info honest so the next person isn't driving out to a closed stand.{% endblocktrans %}
